## Ready to upload: post by hand, with everything prepared

- **Ready page**: each account's next videos with the caption, credit and hashtags ready to copy,
  plus Show file, Open TikTok upload and **Mark as posted** (with Undo). **Copy to a folder** puts the
  videos and a caption file beside each in a folder, for posting from your phone.
- **Watched folders** (Settings → Ready to upload): new clips, from ClipMonster's Automation folder for
  example, come in by themselves, get scored and can be shared out between your accounts.
- **ClipMonster's extras come along**: its hook becomes the caption, its AI rating and quality warnings
  show up, rejected clips are left out, and Creative Commons clips get the credit line their licence needs.
- **Reminders** when a video's posting time comes (accounts without autopilot) and a **morning summary**
  of what's ready.
- Fixed: with more than one account, the Library's filter could start on the separator and fail to load.
