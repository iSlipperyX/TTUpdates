## A posting schedule that matches the research

Studies of millions of TikTok posts show that posting more raises the odds of a hit, while the
typical video gets about the same views at any rate, and TikTok's rules count "unauthorized
automation" as spam. The schedule now follows that:

- **New accounts start at 2 a day, 11:00 to 21:00, at least 3 hours apart** (it was 3 a day from
  09:00, 2 hours apart). Accounts you already have keep their own numbers.
- **Times vary a little each day.** Each post time moves by up to ±15 minutes, differently every
  day and for every account, so nothing goes up at the exact same minute daily. The times stay put
  when the queue is replanned. Set **Vary times** to 0 on the Queue page to keep them exact.
- **What you ask for is what you get.** When more posts are set than fit between the first and last
  time at the minimum gap, they used to be dropped without a word (9 a day from 09:00 to 21:00
  2 hours apart only ever planned 5). Now as many as fit are spread evenly (7 there) and the Queue
  page says so.
- **Warm-up for new TikTok accounts.** Tick **New account: warm up** on the Queue page, or in
  first-run setup: no posts for 3 days while you watch your niche on TikTok, then 1 a day, then 2 a
  day in week two, then your own number. Counted from when the account was added, if that was this
  fortnight.
- **A warning past 3 a day**, the most that posting studies support.
