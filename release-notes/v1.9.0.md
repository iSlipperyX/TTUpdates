## Clips from Google Drive

ClipMonster can upload every clip it finishes to your Google Drive. TikTok Manager can now bring
them in from there: turn it on in **Settings → Google Drive**, sign in, and every few minutes the
clips it hasn't got are downloaded and land in the Library or on the Ready page like clips from a
watched folder, with their titles, scores, hooks, niches and part series.

- **Nothing new to set up in Google.** It signs in with the same Google client ClipMonster uses. On
  the PC ClipMonster runs on that's picked up by itself; on another PC, paste ClipMonster's client ID
  and secret. It only ever sees the files ClipMonster put in your Drive.
- **No doubles.** Clips already in TikTok Manager (from ClipMonster's folder on this PC) aren't
  downloaded again, nor are clips you removed, cleaned up, or that ClipMonster's quality check
  rejected. Each clip taken is marked in Drive with the PC's name, so TikTok Manager on a second PC
  leaves it alone and a clip is never posted twice.
- **Kind to your disk.** Only clips uploaded in the last 14 days are taken (change it in Settings),
  downloads that get cut off carry on where they stopped, and **Clean up posted videos** clears
  them again once they're posted.
- **Always says what's happening.** The Ready page has a ☁ Google Drive line with the last look and a
  **Check now** link, and says plainly what's wrong if something stops it (signed out, no folder,
  disk full).

Clips are downloaded to Videos\TikTok Manager\From Google Drive. The guide is in
[docs/google-drive.md](https://github.com/iSlipperyX/TTUpdates/blob/main/docs/google-drive.md).
