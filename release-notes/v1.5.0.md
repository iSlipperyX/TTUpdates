## Live stats from the official TikTok API

- **New Live page** (between Queue and Account). Once an account is connected to TikTok's official
  API, the app reads it every minute (30 s to 10 min, your choice) and shows today's new views,
  likes, comments, shares and followers, your latest videos with their views in the last hour and a
  trend line, and an activity feed: "+250 views, +12 likes on …", new followers, milestones, a video
  taking off, and a summary of what happened while the app was closed.
- **Posted from your phone? It notices.** When a new video shows up on the account and its caption
  matches a clip in the queue, that clip is marked as posted by itself and leaves the Ready page.
- **Alerts** for new posts, milestones and videos taking off (Settings → TikTok API, on by default).
- **Watch live** can be turned off per account. If the connection expires or TikTok says no, the page
  stops and says why instead of retrying forever; brief errors are retried with a growing wait.
- Stats refreshes use the API too when an account is connected, and fall back to the browser if it's
  down. Live readings don't flood the history: the library keeps one point per half hour.

## Connect each account

- **Connect API** on the Account page approves TikTok Manager in the account's own browser profile, so
  it's always the right account. The page shows who it's connected as and how many days the
  connection has left, with **Test** and **Disconnect**.
- **Post with** in Manage accounts sets how each account posts: Browser, Official TikTok API, or API
  to TikTok's inbox (a draft you finish on your phone). The app-wide "Upload with" setting is gone.
- **Settings → TikTok API** walks you through making the free developer app on developers.tiktok.com,
  step by step, with the redirect address to copy and the permissions to add.

## ClipMonster 2.3 and 2.4 clips

- **Niches go to their account.** A clip from a ClipMonster niche whose account name matches one of
  your accounts (with or without the @) is queued for that account.
- **Part series stay together.** "Part 1 / 2 / 3" clips are queued in order and back to back, also with
  Smart order, and the Ready page and Queue show "Part 2 of 3".
- Clips that ClipMonster's **Clean up** deletes after you posted them keep their stats and history,
  aren't brought in again, and the Library says the file was deleted after posting.
- `results.json` now tells ClipMonster which niche each clip was from.

## Good to know

- TikTok's API only lists **public** videos and has no comment text, so private or "Only me" videos
  don't appear on the Live page.
- A sandbox app works for up to 10 of your own accounts without TikTok's review. Each one has to be
  added under Sandbox settings → Target users first.
- Settings → Updates → Copy diagnostics now says how each account posts and whether the API is set up.
