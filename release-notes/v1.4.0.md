## Post by hand, faster

- **By hand or autopilot, per account.** The Queue page has a **✋ By hand / 🤖 Autopilot** switch
  (also in Manage accounts → Posting). By hand, the schedule's times are reminders and you post from
  the Ready page. The app now opens on the Ready page for an account you post to by hand, and on the
  Queue for one on autopilot.
- **Clips are named by their hook.** The Ready page, Queue, reminders and notifications show
  ClipMonster's hook ("He quit his job live on air") instead of a file name like "01 - 1-37 - clip".
- **Keyboard shortcuts on the Ready page.** J/K or ↑/↓ to move between videos, C copy caption,
  H hashtags, F show file, P play, T ✨ hashtags, E edit, Enter mark as posted, Ctrl+Z undo. The
  outlined card is the one the keys act on; after marking one posted, the next takes over.
- **"N posted today"** next to what's waiting on the Ready page.

## Post from your phone

- **Phone folder**: the next day's clips for the accounts you post to by hand, each with a `.txt` of
  its caption, kept in a folder that syncs to your phone (**OneDrive › TikTok Ready** with one click
  on the Ready page, or any folder in Settings → Ready to upload).
- It keeps itself tidy: posted clips are taken out, reordering renames rather than copies again, and
  a clip you delete on your phone isn't copied back. Its card on the Ready page asks whether you
  posted it or want it back.
- Only files the app put there are touched. Two PCs can share the folder, and it can sit inside a
  watched folder without its copies coming back in as new clips.

## First-run setup

- A new install starts with three short steps: your account and TikTok login, where clips come from
  (ClipMonster's folder is found by itself), and how you post (by hand or autopilot, posts a day,
  phone folder). Existing installs aren't asked. Run it any time from Settings → Ready to upload.

## Fixes and tidying

- **No more "unsupported command-line flag" bar** in the posting browser. The browser used to start
  with `--no-sandbox` (and a flag that hid automation), which Edge warns about. It now starts with
  its normal sandbox and no warned-about flags, and still looks like a normal browser to websites.
  After updating, run **Test posting** on the Account page once to check posting still works.
- Settings and Manage accounts no longer squash their contents on smaller windows (the tabs scroll).
- Settings → Updates → Copy diagnostics now says how many accounts are on autopilot, whether an AI key
  is available (yours or ClipMonster's) and whether the phone folder is on.
- "Copy to a folder" names files by the hook too. Some unused code and an unused setting were removed.
