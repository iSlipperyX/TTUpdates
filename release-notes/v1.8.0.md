## Switch between niches

Gaming clips, finance clips: TikTok Manager can now show one niche at a time. A **niche switcher** sits
next to the account button. Pick **Gaming Videos** and the Library, Ready, Queue and Account pages
show only gaming clips; **All niches** shows everything again.

- **It only changes what you see.** Every queued video still posts at its time, and the phone folder
  keeps all of them.
- **Every clip gets a niche, old ones too.** ClipMonster's word comes first (under the niche's current
  name, so a renamed niche follows). For clips it didn't tag, the niche comes from the niche's folder
  the clip sits in, the other clips from the same video, the niche's channel list in ClipMonster, the
  account the clip is for, or, for clips from before ClipMonster had niches, the YouTube charts (when
  only one niche takes chart videos). The Library has a new **Niche** column, with why in its tooltip.
- **Fix one by hand.** Right-click videos in the Library → **Niche** → pick one, **New niche…**,
  **No niche** or **Work it out again**. What you pick always wins.
- **Each page fits in.** The Ready page says how many more wait in other niches, and **Queue N new
  clips here** takes only the shown niche's. On the Queue, numbers are places in the whole queue, and
  moving or sorting a niche's clips only swaps them among their own places, so other niches keep their
  times. The Account page's views and tables count only that niche's posts.
- The switcher only shows up once the library has niches, and it remembers your pick.
