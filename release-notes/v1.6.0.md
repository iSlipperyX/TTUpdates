## Claude or Gemini

TikTok Manager's AI can now be Google's Gemini as well as Claude, like ClipMonster 2.6.0.

- **Settings → AI → AI to use** picks Claude or Gemini for AI rating and captions, ✨ Hashtags, the
  AI captions and hashtags for new clips from watched folders, and ✨ Suggest bios.
- **Each AI has its own key and model.** Claude uses your Anthropic API key, as before. Gemini uses a
  Gemini API key, free from [aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey), with
  Gemini 4 Argon (best), Gemini 3.8 Flash (the default) or Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (cheapest). Google may
  use what free keys send to improve its products; keys with billing turned on aren't used that way.
- **One key serves both apps.** A key left empty here is borrowed from ClipMonster on this PC, now for
  Gemini too.
- **If it fails, try the other AI** (on by default): with both keys added, a call the chosen AI can't
  answer (a rejected key, a used-up free quota, busy servers) goes to the other one. If the chosen AI
  has no key, the other one is used.
- **Test keys** checks each key and its model without using any tokens.

Nothing changes until you choose Gemini: existing settings keep using Claude.
