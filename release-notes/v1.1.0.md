## Several TikTok accounts in one app

- **Account switcher** at the top left (or Ctrl+1…9). Add as many accounts as you like.
- Every account has its **own login**, **queue**, **schedule**, **autopilot switch**, **stats**, and
  **posting defaults** (who can watch, hashtags on every post, and the niche AI captions are written for).
- The **video library is shared**: queue a video for whichever account should post it. Right-click to
  queue for another account, move a video between queues, or queue a posted video again for a second account.
- **Autopilot posts for every account** that has it switched on, one upload at a time. Turn it on or
  off per account from the Queue page or the tray icon.
- Safety check: before uploading, the app confirms the browser is logged in to the right TikTok account.
- If a scheduled video's file has gone missing, autopilot marks it failed and moves on instead of
  holding up the queue.

Your existing queue, schedule, stats and TikTok login become the first account automatically.
