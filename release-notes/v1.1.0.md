## Several TikTok accounts in one app

- **Account switcher** at the top left (or Ctrl+1…9). Add as many accounts as you like.
- Every account has its **own login**, **queue**, **schedule**, **autopilot switch**, **stats**, and
  **posting defaults** (who can watch, hashtags on every post, and the niche AI captions are written for).
- The **video library is shared**: queue a video for whichever account should post it. Right-click to
  queue for another account, move a video between queues, or queue a posted video again for a second account.
- **Autopilot posts for every account** that has it switched on, one upload at a time. Turn it on or
  off per account from the Queue page or the tray icon.
- Safety check: before uploading, the app confirms the browser is logged in to the right TikTok account.
- If a scheduled video's file has gone missing, autopilot marks it failed and moves on instead of
  holding up the queue.

Your existing queue, schedule, stats and TikTok login become the first account automatically.

## Ready to upload: post by hand, with everything prepared

- **Ready page**: each account's next videos with the caption, credit and hashtags ready to copy,
  plus Show file, Open TikTok upload and **Mark as posted** (with Undo). **Copy to a folder** puts the
  videos and a caption file beside each in a folder, for posting from your phone.
- **Watched folders** (Settings → Ready to upload): new clips, from ClipMonster's Automation folder for
  example, come in by themselves, get scored and can be shared out between your accounts.
- **ClipMonster's extras come along**: its hook becomes the caption, its AI rating and quality warnings
  show up, rejected clips are left out, and Creative Commons clips get the credit line their licence needs.
- **Reminders** when a video's posting time comes (accounts without autopilot) and a **morning summary**
  of what's ready.

## ClipMonster learns from your results

- After each stats refresh, how ClipMonster's clips did (views against your usual) goes back to
  ClipMonster, which then picks more from channels that work and writes hooks like the hits.
- The Account page shows the best and worst source channels and the best hook.
