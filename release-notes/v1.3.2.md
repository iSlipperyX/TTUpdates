## AI hashtags

- New **✨ Hashtags** button on every card on the Ready page, and next to ✨ AI caption when you edit
  a video. Claude writes hashtags for the clip: who is in it (streamer, creator), the game or topic,
  and the kind of moment. They fit the account's niche (Manage accounts → Niche / audience).
- Hashtags learn from your results. Once a few posts have view counts, Claude is told which of your
  hashtags got the most views and reuses them where they fit.
- No spam tags (#fyp, #foryou, #viral and the like) and none the account already adds to every post.
  How many to write is set in Settings → AI → Hashtags per video (4 by default).
- **New clips from ClipMonster get AI hashtags by themselves** (Settings → Ready to upload → Write
  AI hashtags for each new clip, on by default). Clips that already have hashtags keep them.
- **✨ Hashtags for N** on the Ready page does every waiting video that has none yet.
- Each one is a quick text-only call, much cheaper than a full ✨ AI caption.
- No key to add if ClipMonster already has one: TikTok Manager uses ClipMonster's Anthropic key
  when its own is empty (Settings → AI says so). A key typed into TikTok Manager is used instead.
