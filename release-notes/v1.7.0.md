## Clean up posted videos

Posted videos no longer have to sit on your disk. **🧹 Clean up…** on the Library (or **Clean up posted
videos…** on the Account page) clears them out and keeps a small text record of each one.

- **A preview, then a question.** The window lists every posted video with the space it frees. Tick
  the ones to clear, press **Clean up**, and say Yes to the question that follows. Nothing is deleted
  before that.
- **A .txt record of each video** goes to Documents › TikTok Manager › Posted videos › *account*
  (or a folder you pick): title, account, when and how it was posted, its TikTok link, its latest
  views, likes, comments and shares, the caption, length, format, file, score, and for ClipMonster
  clips the channel, source link, niche, hook and why it was picked. No record, no delete.
- **Every trace goes:** the video, its thumbnail, ClipMonster's subtitles and thumbnail for it (and its
  row in ClipMonster's clips.json and README; an emptied video folder goes too, never a watched folder
  or a niche folder), and the copies **Copy to a folder** made.
- **Careful by default.** Videos not seen on TikTok yet start unticked, a file still queued for another
  account stays, **Posted more than N days ago** keeps recent posts, and **Move to the Recycle Bin**
  is there if you'd rather be able to undo it. A file that can't be deleted (open in a player, say) is
  tried again next time.
- **History and stats stay.** Cleaned-up videos stay on the Account page as *Posted · cleaned up*
  (right-click → **Open its record**), ClipMonster keeps learning from their results, and the Library
  shows them only under its new **Cleaned up** filter.
